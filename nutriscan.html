Methodology

How FSSAI INR Rating Works

The Indian Nutrition Rating (INR) translates front-of-pack nutrition parameters into a 0.5 to 5.0 Star score.

1

Baseline Points (Risk Factors)

Calculates risk points based on levels of Energy, Saturated Fat, Total Sugars, and Sodium per 100g/100ml. Higher levels result in more baseline risk points.

2

Positive Points (Healthy Ingredients)

Awards positive points for beneficial components like Fruit, Vegetables, Legumes, Nuts & Millets (FVL%), Dietary Fibre, and Protein content.

3

Star Rating Conversion

Net Score = (Baseline Points) minus (Positive Points). The final score maps strictly into a 0.5 to 5.0 Star visual rating badge.
